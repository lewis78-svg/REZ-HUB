Lesson 1: Introduction to Cybersecurity

1. What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage or theft.

2. Why is Cybersecurity Important?

  • Protects personal and business information.
  • Prevents unauthorized access to computer systems.
  • Helps protect people from online fraud.
  • Improves privacy and data security.

3. Common Cybersecurity Threats

  • Phishing: Fake messages designed to steal information.
  • Malware: Harmful software that can damage devices or steal data.
  • Password attacks: Attempts to guess or steal passwords.
  • Social engineering: Manipulating people into revealing confidential information.

4. Basic Security Practices

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep software and devices updated.
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Only test systems when you have permission.

5. Quick Quiz

Question 1: What is the main purpose of cybersecurity?

A. To damage computers

B. To protect systems and information

C. To steal passwords

Reveal the answer

Correct answer: B. To protect systems and information.

Question 2: What should you do before testing someone else's system?

Reveal the answer

Obtain explicit authorization from the system owner.

Question 3: Name one way to protect an online account.

Reveal the answer

Use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication.
